# React Bits Skill

This directory contains the entire curated code, components, and demos from [DavidHDev/react-bits](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits).

## Available Categories & Components:
- **Animations** (`src/content/Animations/` and `src/tailwind/Animations/`):
  - `AnimatedContent`, `Antigravity`, `BlobCursor`, `ClickSpark`, `Crosshair`, `Cubes`, `CursorGrid`, `ElasticMesh`, `FadeContent`, `FollowCursor`, `GlareCard`, `GradualBlur`, `Magnet`, `MetaBalls`, `Noise`, `PixelCard`, `Ribbons`, `SplashCursor`, `StarBorder`, `TargetCursor`, `WaveRibbon`, and more.
- **Backgrounds** (`src/content/Backgrounds/` and `src/tailwind/Backgrounds/`):
  - `Aurora`, `Ballpit`, `ColorBands`, `DotGrid`, `FaultyTerminal`, `FluidSimulation`, `GridDistortion`, `GridMotion`, `Hyperspeed`, `Iridescence`, `LetterGlitch`, `LightRays`, `Lightning`, `Particles`, `PixelBlast`, `RippleGrid`, `ShaderMotion`, `Squares`, `Threads`, `TrueFocus`, `Waves`, and more.
- **Components** (`src/content/Components/` and `src/tailwind/Components/`):
  - `BounceCards`, `CardNav`, `CircularGallery`, `Counter`, `DecayCard`, `Dock`, `ElasticSlider`, `FlowingMenu`, `GlassIcons`, `InfiniteScroll`, `Lanyard`, `MagicCard`, `Masonry`, `OrbitImages`, `PixelCard`, `PixelTransition`, `RollingGallery`, `ScrollIsland`, `SpotlightCard`, `Stack`, `Stepper`, `TiltedCard`, and more.
- **TextAnimations** (`src/content/TextAnimations/` and `src/tailwind/TextAnimations/`):
  - `AsciiText`, `BlurText`, `CountUp`, `CurvedText`, `DecryptedText`, `FuzzyText`, `GlitchText`, `GradualSpacing`, `LetterGlitch`, `RotatingText`, `ScrollVelocity`, `ShinyText`, `SplitText`, `TrueFocus`, `Typewriter`, `VariableProximity`, `WaveText`, and more.

## Usage Guide:
- Inspect any component in `src/content/<Category>/<ComponentName>/` (standard CSS) or `src/tailwind/<Category>/<ComponentName>/` (Tailwind variant).
- Check `src/constants/Components.js` and `src/constants/Information.js` for props, dependencies, and metadata.
